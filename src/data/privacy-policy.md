## Data Collection and Storage

When you use the contact form on this website, the information you provide (such as your name,
email address, phone number, and message content) is collected to facilitate communication regarding
potential projects or collaborations.

Please be aware that this data is stored in a **private, secure database** maintained
by Pablo Sierra. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal
information is handled responsibly. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally
identifiable information to outside parties.

and confirm the action via email.

## Data Audit

Users have the right to request an audit of their data. Upon receiving such a request, we will
take the possibility of an audit into consideration.

Any data audit would be conducted by an external third-party company. This company must be
mutually agreed upon by both the requester and Pablo Sierra prior to the commencement of any works.
Additionally, the allocation of costs associated with the audit must be defined and agreed upon
in advance.

The entire process regarding data audits will be managed via email. To initiate this process,
you may send a request through our [contact form](/contact).
